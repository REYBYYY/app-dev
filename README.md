## 📖 Plot Summary

**Sean Boswell**, a troubled teenager with a passion for street racing, gets sent to live with his father in **Tokyo, Japan** after wrecking cars one too many times. There, he discovers the underground world of **drift racing** on the winding mountain roads of Tokyo. With the help of his new friend **Twinkie** and the legendary drifter **Han**, Sean learns the art of drifting and finds himself caught up in a dangerous race against the **Yakuza**.

## 🏁 Fun Facts

1. The movie was filmed **primarily on location in Tokyo**
2. **Sung Kang** (Han) became so popular that they wrote him into **earlier films** as a prequel
3. The famous *"I live my life a quarter mile at a time"* line is referenced in **every** Fast & Furious movie
4. Many of the drift scenes used **professional drift drivers** instead of the actors
